# Research on Oxford Nanopore Technologies

Roughly 50 years ago, the technologies to read genetic code were developed. This was known as Sanger sequencing, named after and developed by Frederick Sanger. Now, in the present time, sequencing technologies have expanded and grown exponentially, with many companies worldwide using sequencing technologies. One of them is Oxford Nanopore Technologies. 

## Oxford Nanopore Technologies Origins

Oxford Nanopore Technologies, originally Oxford Nanolabs, was founded in 2005 by Dr Gordon Sanghera, Dr Spike Willcocks, and Professor Hagan Bayley.
